# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project serves family caregivers — people often under significant emotional and physical stress. We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, language, level of experience, nationality, religion, or any other characteristic.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviors:**
- Being patient with non-native English speakers (this project's community spans Chinese, Indonesian, and English speakers)
- Respecting that contributors may be active caregivers with limited time
- Giving constructive feedback with empathy
- Focusing on what is best for caregivers and patients

**Unacceptable behaviors:**
- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Dismissing contributions because of language barriers
- Publishing others' private information
- Giving medical advice beyond the project's record-keeping scope

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue or contacting the repository owner. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated.
